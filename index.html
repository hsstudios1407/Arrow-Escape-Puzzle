# Arrow-Escape-Puzzle Privacy Policy - Arrow Escape Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Arrow Escape Puzzle

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

HS Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Arrow Escape Puzzle mobile game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and shared when you use our game.

2. Information We Collect

Arrow Escape Puzzle does not require you to create an account or provide personal information directly to us to play the game.

The game may store gameplay information locally on your device, such as game progress, levels, coins, stars, hints, and other game preferences.

3. Advertising

Arrow Escape Puzzle uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and information about ad interactions in accordance with Google's policies.

Advertisements may include banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and rewarded advertisements.

For more information about how Google uses information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Rewarded Advertisements

The game may offer optional rewarded advertisements. Users may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward, such as an extra life, hint, undo, or star.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape Puzzle is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

6. Third-Party Services

The game uses third-party services, including Google AdMob, which may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the game. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Arrow Escape Puzzle, you can contact HS Studios through the developer contact information provided on Google Play.
